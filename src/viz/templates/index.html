About the entries

The papers come from PubMed Central (PMC), mostly from Acta Crystallographica Section E (Acta E). Each entry links a CSD refcode to a procedure extracted from its paper. We include the source text and, where available, the publisher’s CIFs and chemical schemes. These files can cover several compounds but in cases where there are multiple structures from a paper, we have not matched individual refcodes to individual structures inside each CIF.
