Publisher sources, across journals

The entries come from Acta Crystallographica Section E (Acta E) and from the other journals in the extraction. Each entry pairs the extracted procedure with its paper, excerpts of the paper's text, and the publisher's CIF and chemical scheme where the archive holds them. A CIF belongs to the whole paper, so its structure blocks are not matched to individual refcodes automatically.

The corpus

Paper identities and publication years come from publisher records and Europe PMC. The charts describe the papers and refcodes in this snapshot.

Licence labels come from the publisher records. A paper whose record names no licence is labelled Not indexed, and the less common labels are grouped under Other.
